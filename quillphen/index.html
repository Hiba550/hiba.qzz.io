The studio

We each handle the part we’re best at.

QuillPhen builds the gameplay systems and handles testing, releases and player support. 0x4a4b builds the visual side: PBR materials, textures, RTX work and presentation. We publish our work, keep it updated, and use player feedback to fix rough edges after release.

02 Released Add-On QuillPhen

Bedrock Add-On · QuillPhen

Build Echo

Build Echo remembers player-built blocks lost to creeper and TNT explosions. It leaves holographic guides in the missing positions, preserves supported block states, and lets the player repair the build with the correct materials.

Authored coverage
79 shape and state variants
Persistence
Echoes survive reloads
Restoration
Individual or batch repair
  • Preserves facing, connection and block-state details
  • Turns destruction into a readable recovery workflow
  • Designed for survival play rather than a creative-only demo
